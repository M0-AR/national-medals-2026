📖 The story in plain language

A ceremony honored six builders of the modern computing stack. Four received the National Medal of Science (new ideas that changed what computers can do). Two received the National Medal of Technology and Innovation (getting those ideas into everyone's hands at scale). The same day, a NASA crew returned from space, and the government described research, quantum, nuclear and moon/Mars plans. This site separates confirmed facts from stories that still need proof, using only public records you can open yourself.

🚀 New primitives
Musk · Brin · Huang · Su — rockets, search, GPUs, chips. Without them, today's AI does not exist.
🌍 Scale + deploy
Dell · Nadella — personal computers and cloud that put those primitives everywhere.
🔬 Policy backdrop
Accord (Sept) → Summit + medals (Oct 8) → 297 Genesis research projects.
